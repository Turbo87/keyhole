Keyhole signed-in application: UX directions

Each direction is a clickable wireframe of the whole staff, admin and scorer application. They share one in-memory dataset and the same leaf forms, and differ in page inventory, route structure, navigation and layout. Nothing persists: reload a direction to reset its data.

Prototype bar

The black bar at the bottom of every direction is not part of the design. It switches between directions (also with ← and →), shows the app as Staff (Mara Keller), Competition admin (Jonas Weber) or Scorer (Lena Fischer), and opens the direction's sitemap with the current URL and route.
